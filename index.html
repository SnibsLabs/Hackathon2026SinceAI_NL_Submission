Why can Border Points be red (Exceeded) when Exposed National Stations is 0? The 22 Border Points check sovereign cross-border field strength across all bands (including nationwide MNO 5G/LTE block licenses like 3500 MHz or 700/800 MHz that are not listed as individual stations in the 874-station Excel file). The Exposed Finnish National Stations table specifically checks co-channel/adjacent stations inside the 874-station Excel registry (try Vyborg UHF 618 MHz or Murmansk FM 87.5 MHz to see both tables light up).
